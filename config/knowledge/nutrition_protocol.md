# Nutrition, Supplementation & Daily Protocols

## Core Dietary Principles
* **Whole Food Base:** Focus on dense, whole food nutrition, unprocessed fats, and quality protein sources.
* **Key Fat & Food Sources:** Frequent consumption of peanut butter, raw organic coconut meat, and cold-pressed coconut oil for sustainable daily energy.

## Energy & Caffeine Protocol
* **Tea-Based Routine:** Transitioned from coffee to a structured tea-based caffeine routine for smooth, steady alertness without energy crashes or jitters.

## Daily Supplementation Framework
* **Daytime Performance Stack:** Formulated for mental focus, cellular energy, and athletic performance during training windows.
* **Nightly Recovery Stack:** Formulated for muscle relaxation, physical recovery, and sleep quality.
