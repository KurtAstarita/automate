# Nutrition Protocol: Integrated Physique & Performance Framework

## 1. Core Principles & Caloric Management

* **Foundational Priority**: Nutrition accounts for the vast majority of physique transformation and metabolic health, driving body composition, cellular recovery, and hormone regulation.
* **Somatotype-Based Caloric & Protein Baselines**:
* **Ectomorph**: Higher metabolic rate and carbohydrate tolerance. Daily protein: **1.3–1.6 g/lb** of body weight; meal frequency: every **2–3 hours**.
* **Mesomorph**: Athletic response to training and balanced nutrient partitioning. Daily protein: **1.2–1.5 g/lb** of body weight; meal frequency: every **2.5–3.5 hours**.
* **Endomorph**: Higher insulin sensitivity considerations and lower carbohydrate tolerance. Daily protein: **1.4–1.7 g/lb** of body weight; meal frequency: every **3.5–5 hours**.


* **Caloric Deficit & Baseline Calculation**:
* Baseline calories are calculated using Lean Body Mass (LBM) and activity levels (e.g., 6–12% body fat establishes a baseline around **17 kcal/lb** of LBM).
* Target body fat loss (~1.5 lbs per week) requires a **600 kcal daily deficit** relative to baseline, balanced between caloric reduction and physical expenditure.


* **Hydration Standards**:
* Baseline hydration targets **1–2 gallons daily** of clean water for active individuals to support waste excretion, glycogen storage, and cellular function. Electrolytes should be prioritized alongside high fluid intake.

## 2. Macronutrient Guidelines & Gut-Conscious Food Selection

* **Protein Requirements**:
* Target **1.0–1.5 g/lb** of total body weight daily depending on training intensity and metabolic demands.
* Prioritize bioavailable complete proteins supplying essential amino acids: eggs, poultry, grass-fed beef, wild fish, organ meats, whey isolate, and cottage cheese.
* Distribute protein evenly across meals (**20–40g per meal**) to maximize muscle protein synthesis and optimize digestion.


* **Dietary Fats & Lipid Integrity**:
* **Prioritized Fats**: Focus on stable monounsaturated and natural saturated fats, including extra virgin olive oil, avocado, macadamia nuts, raw organic coconut meat, cold-pressed coconut oil, and quality animal fats (grass-fed butter, tallow).
* **Saturated Fat Role**: Saturated fats serve as critical structural components for cell membranes and foundational precursors for steroid hormone production (testosterone).
* **Strict Seed Oil Elimination**: Completely remove industrial seed and vegetable oils (canola, soybean, corn, sunflower, cottonseed). High levels of easily oxidized omega-6 polyunsaturated fats in these oils generate toxic lipid peroxides and systemic inflammation.


* **Carbohydrates, Anti-Nutrients & Gut Health**:
* **Low-Antinutrient Starches**: Emphasize clean, easily digestible carbohydrates like white jasmine rice, cooked tubers (sweet potatoes), and traditional long-fermentation sourdough bread. Jasmine rice provides immediate glucose for muscle glycogen replenishment without causing intestinal distress.
* **Sourdough & Fermentation**: Long-fermentation sourdough utilizes beneficial microbes to neutralize phytic acid and pre-digest gluten, improving mineral absorption and digestibility.
* **Eliminate Intestinal Irritants**: Avoid heavy bran fractions, unfermented whole grains, and raw oats. The coarse insoluble fiber, phytates, and lectins in whole grain husks can irritate the gut mucosa (exacerbating hyperpermeability/leaky gut) and bind to vital minerals like zinc, magnesium, and calcium.


* **Micronutrients & Digestion**:
* Incorporate low-FODMAP, well-cooked vegetables and nutrient-dense fruits to supply essential water-soluble and fat-soluble vitamins without causing GI distress.

## 3. Meal Timing, Frequency & Hormonal Optimization

* **Meal Structure & Prep**:
* Divide daily nutrition into **4–6 structured meals** to ensure steady amino acid availability, balanced energy levels, and optimal nutrient absorption.
* **Breakfast**: Restores glycogen, halts overnight catabolism, and establishes positive nitrogen balance for the day.
* Precision tracking via weighing foods raw or cooked ensures strict alignment with caloric goals.


* **Hormonal Regulation**:
* **Insulin Management**: Direct carbohydrate intake around training windows (pre/post-workout) to drive muscle glycogen replenishment and nutrient transport into skeletal muscle while keeping baseline insulin stable.
* **Cortisol Control**: Prevent catabolic cortisol spikes through structured sleep (**8–10 hours**), strategic intra/post-workout carbohydrate consumption, and managing physical stress.
* **Anabolic Optimization**: Support natural testosterone and growth hormone production with adequate dietary saturated/monounsaturated fats, progressive strength training, and deep sleep cycles.

## 4. Supplementation Protocols

* **Baseline Protocol**:
* Treat supplements as targeted additions to a solid, whole-food diet. Consult baseline blood work to address individual micronutrient gaps.


* **Creatine Monohydrate**:
* **Function**: Restores intracellular ATP stores during high-intensity, explosive movement.
* **Dosing**: Optional loading phase of **20g daily for 5 days**, followed by a consistent **5g daily** maintenance dose taken post-workout with carbohydrates to maximize cellular uptake.


* **Protein Powders**:
* **Whey Protein Isolate**: Highly bioavailable, rapidly digested, and virtually free of lactose and fat, making it gentle on digestion post-workout.
* **Slow-Digesting Proteins**: Cottage cheese, casein, or collagen peptides for sustained amino acid delivery.


* **Amino Acids & Mineral Support**:
* **BCAAs / EAAs**: Useful during fasted states or long training sessions; leucine acts as the key trigger for protein synthesis.
* **L-Glutamine**: Supports intestinal mucosal integrity and muscle recovery under heavy training loads.
* **ZMA / Magnesium**: Zinc and magnesium support hormonal recovery, nervous system down-regulation, and cellular repair overnight.
